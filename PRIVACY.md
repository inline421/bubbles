# Privacy Policy — Playtime

**Last updated: 28 September 2026**

## The short version

Playtime collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no
analytics, and it makes no network requests at all. Photos you add stay on
your own device and are never uploaded anywhere.

## The complete version

**What we collect:** Nothing. Playtime does not collect, transmit, or store
any personal information from anyone, of any age, anywhere other than on your
own device.

**Network activity:** None. Playtime makes no network requests of any kind.
Once loaded it functions entirely offline. It does not contact us and it does
not contact anyone else.

**Photos.** This is the part worth being precise about.

- Photos are chosen by a parent through the device's own file picker.
- They are stored in the browser's local database **on that device only**.
- They are **never uploaded**, never transmitted, never backed up to any
  server, and never included in the app's public source code repository.
- We cannot see them. Nobody but the person holding the device can see them.
- Deleting the app removes them.
- The app never accesses your photo library on its own — it only receives the
  specific files a parent selects.

**Third parties:** There are none. Playtime contains no third-party libraries,
advertising networks, analytics services, or crash reporting. No data is
shared with anyone because no data leaves the device.

**Device storage:** Playtime stores your chosen photos and small display
preferences locally. This never leaves the device and is removed when the app
is deleted. Note that the operating system may clear local storage for apps it
considers unused; if that happens, photos can simply be added again.

**Advertising and tracking:** None. Playtime contains no advertising, performs
no tracking, and does not access any advertising identifier.

**Children's privacy:** Playtime is designed for young children. Because it
collects no personal information and transmits nothing, there is nothing to
consent to under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU
General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), or comparable laws. It was designed
this way deliberately.

**Data retention and deletion:** We hold no data about you, so there is nothing
to retain or delete on our side. Photos and preferences are removed by deleting
the app.

**Changes to this policy:** If Playtime ever changes in a way that affects this
policy, we will update this page and the date above.
