Playtime — Recorder

Record the sounds in your own voice. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — each take saves straight to your Downloads folder and stays on this machine.

Before you start: quiet room, phone or laptop about a hand's width from your mouth, and leave a beat of silence at the start and end of each take — I trim that off automatically. Re-record as many times as you like; only the last take of each is saved.

The bar should jump when you talk. If it stays flat, the wrong input is selected.
