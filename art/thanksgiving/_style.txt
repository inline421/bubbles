House style, read off the existing stickers rather than guessed at.

  outline   near-black #22201e. 5 units on a 0 0 200 200 box for the
            silhouette, 4 for interior divisions, 3 for fine detail.
            Round caps and joins.

  three     every form carries a BASE colour, a DARKER shade on the side
  tones     away from the light, and a PALE panel on the face of it. The
            lion's muzzle, belly and paw tips; the cow's snout. That pale
            panel is the thing the first version of this set was missing,
            and it is most of why the originals read as drawn and the first
            seasonal set read as flat.

  faces     black oval eyes, a small mouth, optional pink cheeks. Anything
            alive gets all three.

  framing   the silhouette fills the box - about 176 of 200 across its long
            dimension. Nothing thin, nothing floating in dead space.

  shading   the light and dark panels are CURVED shapes clipped to the
            silhouette, never a straight seam down the middle. A ruled split
            reads as a 3D render; none of the existing stickers do it.

Two renderer traps, both found the hard way:

  * cairosvg will not let a child override an inherited stroke-linecap.
    Anything needing butt caps - the candy cane's dashed stripes - must sit
    outside a group that sets round, which is what write_plain() is for.
    Round caps add half the stroke width to each end of every dash, so at
    44 wide they swallow a 20-unit gap whole and the stripes vanish.

  * clipPath works on fills here but not on strokes. Panels are clipped;
    stroked stripes are not.

Sources are the .svg files beside this note. draw_xmas.py and draw_thanks.py
in art2/ regenerate them; render.py turns them into the webp the app loads.
